## Important governance note

These entries do **not** claim that Warek I has physically reviewed or signed the decision sheet.

For planning purposes, the Product Owner instructed the project to assume that Warek I selects the most rational, operationally simple options that support a two-week V1 delivery target.

Any later real institutional correction must be recorded explicitly and assessed for scope/timeline impact.

## Scope-control rule

Any requested change that introduces:
- complex refund automation;
- dynamic pricing;
- multiple mandatory review rounds;
- full online/hybrid infrastructure;
- publication automation;
- expanded certificate design tooling;
- other non-core engines

is treated as a post-V1 change unless explicitly accepted with timeline impact.
