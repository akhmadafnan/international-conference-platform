# Project Charter — ICHES Conference & Event Experience Platform

**ID:** ICHES-GOV-CHARTER-001
**Status:** HISTORICAL FOUNDATION CHARTER / PURPOSE PRESERVED
**Updated:** 2026-10-03

## Purpose

Build a reusable, multilingual academic conference platform that is operationally simple for a small-to-medium edition while remaining structurally sound for recurring editions.

## Initial operating context

- roughly 100 participants/submissions;
- participant base expected to be primarily Indonesian initially;
- limited committee and technical resources;
- recurring annual/biennial or configurable editions;
- one platform intended to preserve historical editions.

## Product boundaries

The product is responsible for:
- public conference information;
- registration and payment;
- participant dashboard;
- abstract/full-article lifecycle;
- review and academic decision support;
- scheduling and event-day operations;
- documents and communication;
- presentation assessment and revision;
- publication-ready scholarly metadata and handoff;
- awards and certificates;
- historical edition records.

The product is not intended to become:
- full ERP;
- OJS replacement;
- hotel/travel platform;
- full MoU contract system;
- automatic academic/award decision engine.

## Non-negotiable principles

- multi-edition;
- id/en/ar with Arabic RTL;
- simple CRUD where sufficient;
- state-aware participant UX;
- human academic/committee authority;
- auditable consequential decisions;
- OJS downstream;
- canonical scholarly metadata;
- standards-oriented OJS/Crossref handoff;
- no one-event disposable schema.
