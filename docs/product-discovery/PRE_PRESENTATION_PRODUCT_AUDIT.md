## Product gaps found and resolved

- LoA separated from room assignment.
- LoA separated from publication acceptance.
- Full Article placed before Scheduling Pool.
- Actual presenter distinguished from corresponding author.
- Attendance distinguished from PRESENTED.
- System award ranking separated from Committee decision.
- Best Article recipients clarified as all authors, individual certificates.
- Participant and Presenter certificates allowed simultaneously.
- Schedule established as database data, not a PDF-only artifact.
- Selected Journal distinguished from journal acceptance.
- Series distinguished from Edition.
- Public site and authenticated dashboard aligned as one product journey.

## Risks if not decided before implementation

- ambiguous refund/participation behavior;
- inconsistent pricing model;
- reviewer workload instability;
- accidental permission overreach;
- incorrect publication claims;
- rework in payment, scheduling, and document modules.

## Scope discipline

V1 must not expand into:
- hotel booking;
- travel management;
- full contract lifecycle;
- OJS replacement;
- AI scheduler;
- AI award winner;
- complex refund engine;
- drag-and-drop certificate design suite;
- microservices;
- full helpdesk/ticketing.
